# CON — Espinas dendríticas y plasticidad estructural

## En una línea
Dendritic spines grow with LTP and shrink with LTD, so synaptic strength has a structural correlate.

## Desarrollo
Using two-photon glutamate uncaging on single spines, Matsuzaki and colleagues (2004) found that repeated stimulation of one spine caused a rapid and persistent enlargement of its head, restricted to the stimulated spine and dependent on NMDA receptors. Spine enlargement involves remodelling of the actin cytoskeleton. Larger heads hold more AMPA receptors and a larger postsynaptic density, which links the molecular and structural views of potentiation.
