# CON — Etiquetado y captura sináptica

## En una línea
Synaptic tagging and capture: a weak stimulus sets a local tag at a synapse, and plasticity-related proteins (PRPs) produced after a strong stimulus elsewhere are captured by every tagged synapse.

## Desarrollo
Frey and Morris (1997) showed that a weak tetanus that normally induces only early LTP in one CA1 pathway can become long-lasting if a strong tetanus is delivered to a second pathway of the same neurons within about 1 to 2 hours. The tag is set independently of protein synthesis; the PRPs come from the soma or from local translation. The model explains how late-phase plasticity can be input-specific even though protein synthesis is cell-wide.
